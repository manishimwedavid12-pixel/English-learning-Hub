📖 English Grammar

Learn important grammar topics.

1. Simple Present Tense

We use the simple present to talk about habits, routines and general facts.

Examples:
I go to school every day.
She studies English.
The sun rises in the east.

2. Present Continuous

We use it for actions happening now.

Examples:
I am studying English.
She is reading a book.
They are playing football.

3. Simple Past

We use the simple past for completed actions in the past.

Examples:
I visited Kigali yesterday.
She studied English last night.

4. Simple Future

We use will to talk about future actions.

Examples:
I will study tomorrow.
They will visit us next week.

5. Nouns

A noun is the name of a person, place, animal, thing or idea.

Examples: teacher, school, book, Rwanda, happiness

6. Pronouns

Pronouns replace nouns.

I, you, he, she, it, we, they

7. Adjectives

Adjectives describe nouns.

Examples:
a beautiful girl
a big house
an intelligent student

8. Adverbs

Adverbs describe verbs, adjectives or other adverbs.

Examples:
He runs quickly.
She speaks slowly.

9. Prepositions

Prepositions show relationships between words.

in, on, at, under, beside, between

10. Conjunctions

Conjunctions connect words or sentences.

and, but, because, or, although

📚 English Vocabulary

Search and learn useful English words.

Achieve

Meaning: To successfully complete something.

Example: I want to achieve my goals.

Brave

Meaning: Not afraid of difficult situations.

Example: The brave student answered.

Improve

Meaning: To become better.

Example: I want to improve my English.

Knowledge

Meaning: Information and understanding.

Example: Education gives us knowledge.

Opportunity

Meaning: A good chance to do something.

Example: Education gives opportunities.

Successful

Meaning: Having achieved a desired result.

Example: She is a successful student.

Challenge

Meaning: Something difficult that tests ability.

Example: Learning English is a challenge.

Confident

Meaning: Feeling sure about your abilities.

Example: She is confident when speaking.
